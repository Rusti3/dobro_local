# Garden assets

Selected models and their texture dependencies from Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit (Standard), supplied locally in `D:\snorks2\assets`.

License: CC0 1.0. The original license is preserved in `nature/License_Standard.txt`.
Only the 12 models used by the garden and their shared dependencies are included.
The scene loads them on entering the garden; instances reuse geometry and textures.

`garden-landscape.png` was the first generated full-screen backdrop.

`garden-landscape-v2.png` is the active backdrop, generated with the built-in GPT Image tool. It preserves the softly blurred stylized countryside, cliffs and turquoise stream while turning the broad central meadow into a crisp foreground surface matched to the low-poly garden assets. The center intentionally contains no baked-in furniture or trees: live 3D objects are placed directly onto the grass, without a floating platform.
