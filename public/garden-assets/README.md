# Garden assets

Selected models and their texture dependencies from Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit (Standard), supplied locally in `D:\snorks2\assets`.

License: CC0 1.0. The original license is preserved in `nature/License_Standard.txt`.
Only the 12 models used by the garden and their shared dependencies are included.
The scene loads them on entering the garden; instances reuse geometry and textures.

`garden-landscape.png` was generated with the built-in GPT Image tool as a full-screen backdrop. Prompt: cozy stylized 3D countryside, elevated isometric view, softly blurred green forest and stone terraces surrounding a low-detail central meadow, winding turquoise stream, warm morning light, full-bleed portrait 1024x1536, no UI or text. The sharp interactive island is rendered separately over this image.
